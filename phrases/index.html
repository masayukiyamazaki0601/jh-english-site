📖 英熟語・重要表現集

中学英語で必須の熟語と会話表現をまとめました。例文と一緒に覚えましょう。

基本動詞を使った熟語

熟語意味例文
get up起きるI get up at seven.
get on / off乗る／降りるGet on the bus.
look at〜を見るLook at the blackboard.
look for〜を探すI'm looking for my pen.
look after〜の世話をするLook after your sister.
look like〜のように見えるIt looks like rain.
listen to〜を聞くListen to the music.
wait for〜を待つWait for me.
ask for〜を求めるAsk for help.
put on着るPut on your coat.
take off脱ぐ／離陸するTake off your shoes.
turn on / offつける／消すTurn on the light.
talk to / with〜と話すTalk to your teacher.
think of / about〜について考えるWhat do you think of it?
be afraid of〜を怖がるI'm afraid of dogs.
be good at〜が得意だShe is good at tennis.
be interested in〜に興味があるI'm interested in history.

会話表現

表現意味使い方
How are you?元気ですか？I'm fine, thank you.
Nice to meet you.はじめましてNice to meet you, too.
Thank you.ありがとうYou're welcome.
I'm sorry.ごめんなさいThat's OK.
Excuse me.すみません呼びかけ・謝罪の両方で使える
Let's 〜.〜しましょうLet's go.
How about 〜?〜はどうですか？How about some coffee?
What's the matter?どうしたの？相手の心配を尋ねる
I think that 〜.〜だと思いますI think that he is right.
I don't know.わかりません丁寧に言うなら I'm not sure.

前置詞と一緒に覚える熟語

熟語意味例
at first最初はAt first, it was difficult.
at lastついにAt last, I found it.
at least少なくともAt least 10 people came.
in fact実際にはIn fact, it's true.
in a hurry急いでI was in a hurry.
on time時間通りにPlease come on time.
on the way途中でOn the way home.
all right大丈夫That's all right.
a lot ofたくさんのa lot of people
a little少しa little water

不規則動詞を含む重要熟語

熟語意味例
go shopping買い物に行くI went shopping yesterday.
go swimming泳ぎに行くLet's go swimming.
have a good time楽しく過ごすWe had a good time.
have lunch昼食を食べるI have lunch at noon.
have a cold風邪をひくI have a cold.
make sure確認するMake sure the door is locked.
make friends友達を作るI made many friends.
take care of世話をするTake care of yourself.
take a walk散歩するLet's take a walk.
take part in参加するTake part in the event.
come true実現するMy dream came true.
give upあきらめるDon't give up.

💡 熟語の覚え方

① 熟語を1つ覚えるたびに、その熟語を使った例文を声に出して読む
② 前置詞（at, in, on, for など）のイメージを理解する（at = 点、in = 中、on = 接触）
③ 動詞ごとにグループ化して覚える（get系、look系、take系など）
④ 毎日5つずつでOK。続けることが最も重要
